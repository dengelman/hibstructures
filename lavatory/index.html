Why fleets rent it at a higher rate.

Private, full-height, warm

Every unit is private rooms with a flushing toilet and a sink, not a trailer with partitions. Full-height doors, hot water, heated and lit. Rated to −40 with heat-traced lines and insulated tanks.

Built like a building

Vinyl-covered panel walls, heat-welded sheet vinyl floor coved up the wall, steel siding and fascias, membrane roof. HIB graphite finish with the amber door surround. Colour-matched repair panels stocked.

Fleet-ready

ISO footprint, corner castings, forkliftable, one connection panel for power, water and pump-out. Direct connect or self-contained. Built on the line that has delivered this programme to Canada's largest rental fleet.
