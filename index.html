By Honomobo.

HIB is the industrial line of Honomobo Corporation, which has built premium steel-frame modular homes in Nisku, Alberta since 2015 and delivers across Canada and the western US. Same factory, same thermally broken frame and envelope. We're a manufacturer: we don't rent, operate or compete with the people who buy from us.
