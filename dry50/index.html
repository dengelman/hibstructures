Dry 50

Three-module mine dry for 50. Coveralls hoisted into an 18' bay, ten private showers, fifty full-height lockers, warm floor throughout. Built in Nisku, Alberta.

Dirty in. Clean out.

1 · Dirty

In under the canopy, boot wash in the lane, boots on a heated rack. Coveralls into a basket and hoisted 12–15' into the warmest air in the building. Double-height module with a clerestory.

2 · Wet

Ten private full-height shower stalls, towel and drying zones, then sinks and toilets on the way out. Sloped, membraned floor, negative pressure.

3 · Clean

Fifty full-height lockers, benches, a 5' aisle, lamp and gas-monitor charging at the door, first aid. Quiet room, out under the camp-side canopy.

Floor plan

Mine side at the top, camp side at the bottom. Two 4' cross-lanes carry every door; the walls between modules carry the plumbing. A men's and women's version uses the same shell with one partition.

How it's built

Building36' × 56', three 12 × 56 steel-frame modules mated on their long edges. Dirty module is a stacked pair, 18' clear.
FrameWelded steel moment frame, bays 16–12–12–16, thermally broken, third-party structural stamp.
EnvelopeR-24 walls, R-30 roof, insulated floor, airtight, rated to −40 °C. Flat steel fascia, vertical corrugated siding, factory finish.
FloorInsulated wood floor system on the steel frame, in-floor heat, heat-welded sheet vinyl with 6" coved base; membrane and drains in wet zones.
ServicesBoiler, HRV and water heater in one mechanical room on the connection wall. Direct connect or heat-traced tanks.
ShippingFour pieces, 11'6" high, standard step decks. Craned to piers, mated, connected at one wall.
