Industrial buildings by Honomobo

A lavatory, a dry, an office or a data hall. Built in Nisku.

Every HIB module is a welded, thermally broken steel-frame building, built in Nisku, Alberta, and delivered on a step deck. Sold to the companies that own and rent site fleets, and to the operators who are done with sea-cans.

From order to on site

  1. Pick the unit and optionsOne spec sheet per product. No custom engineering unless you want it.
  2. Prepare the siteWe issue the pier, utility and crane guide; your engineer signs off.
  3. Factory buildBuilt and tested in Nisku, Alberta. Target lead time 10 weeks for Lavatory, 12 for Dry, 16 for Compute.
  4. Deliver and dockStep-deck delivery, craned to piers, connected at one wall.

Pricing: fixed per unit, quoted per product and option set. Fleet pricing and lease financing available through partners.
Warranty: structure and envelope 10 years; fixtures and systems per manufacturer, coordinated by HIB.
Origin: built in Canada by Honomobo Corporation, Nisku, Alberta. Manufacturer to fleet owners and integrators since 2015.
